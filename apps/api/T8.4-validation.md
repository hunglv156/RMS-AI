## Delivered

- Registered feature controllers through the existing jobs and JD module stubs. Reused T8.2's global session/CSRF guard, explicit action policy, job/resource authorization, and transactional audit writer.
- Job creation, detail, title/status updates with revision conflicts, bounded pagination, stable sorting, literal title search and status filtering. Reviewer list rows and totals are restricted by assignment; inaccessible detail IDs return 404.
- Admin-only atomic replacement of reviewer assignments. Grants accept only existing active reviewers, reject duplicates, and serialize with account administration to avoid role/disable races during validation.
- JD version list/detail/create, full draft edits, submit, return to draft with a required reason, approve, and clone. Mutations check expected revision; non-draft edits and invalid state transitions return 409. Recruiters author/submit/clone but cannot approve; assigned reviewers and admins approve/return; unassigned reviewers cannot inspect or approve versions.
- Strict criteria validation: 1–8 entries, unique nonempty codes, required label/description/priority, finite nonnegative weights and a finite positive total. Keywords and priority map directly to the frozen AI contract. Approval stores a stable SHA-256 content hash and approver/timestamp.
- Job-first row locking serializes version allocation, content changes, approvals, job closure and assignment replacement. Approval freezes the version and updates active JD/job revision in the same transaction as audit. Concurrent approvals of distinct versions both remain in history; the final serialized approval becomes active. Concurrent approval of the same revision yields one success and one conflict.
- Existing foundation database guards protect approved content/criteria under the restricted application role; no migration was needed. Fault injection proves an audit failure rolls back both approval and active-JD update.

## Interpretations and approved scope exception

“CRUD metadata” follows the plan's explicit routes: create/read/update, close/reopen, and no destructive domain DELETE endpoint. Lists use `{items,total,page,pageSize}`, default page size 20 and maximum 100; job sorting supports title/created_at and JD sorting also supports version_no, with an ID tie-breaker. Draft edits replace the structured content/criteria as required by the frozen UpdateJd DTO.

All submitted draft content is validated at create/edit and revalidated at submit/approve. Clone accepts the source revision and allocates a fresh draft version under the job lock; it copies content only and clears submission/approval/hash metadata. Version creation and cloning have no expected job revision field in the frozen contract; row locks and unique constraints protect allocation. Assignment replacement likewise follows the frozen `{user_ids}` contract and is serialized, without inventing a required revision field.

Closed jobs retain reads and draft/history operations; approval is blocked until reopened, as section 4 specifies. Approved historical versions remain immutable and inspectable even after another version becomes active. The hash identifies ordered structured content, excludes workflow metadata/UUIDs, and preserves criterion order for future snapshots.

The coordinator explicitly approved extending `apps/api/src/audit/audit-writer.service.ts` and `apps/api/test/audit/audit.integration.test.ts` outside the original T8.4 directories (question `msg_70db38a40ab8`, reply `msg_004f7aac4f3d`). The extension permits only trimmed 1–2000-character `return_reason` metadata on `jd.returned_to_draft`; all other actions retain their rejection of free-text metadata. This reconciles the required audit reason with the frozen ReturnToDraft request without modifying contracts/schema or weakening unrelated audit events.

No remaining T8.4 blocker. Web workflow integration, candidate/application functionality, screening, dashboard and audit-reader UI remain with their listed later owners.
